WHY THIS WORKS

Gold only ever marks what is live or actionable — the active tab, the pin, a fit score, the section rules. Crimson is the ground and the blood accent; it never carries body text, so nothing smears together at 13px.

Bone is the reading colour everywhere, which keeps the contrast above 12:1 on oxblood. Square corners and 1px hairlines replace the old pill shapes, so the interface reads as engraved rather than bubbly.
